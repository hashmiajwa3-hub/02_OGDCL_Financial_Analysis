Who Is OGDCL?
Pakistan's national energy champion, with over six decades of exploration and production experience across the country
Company Profile

Oil and Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL) is Pakistan's largest state-owned Exploration and Production company. Founded in 1961, OGDCL has grown into the backbone of Pakistan's energy sector, contributing approximately 42% of Pakistan's total crude oil and 48% of natural gas production every year. The Government of Pakistan holds approximately 69% equity through the Privatization Commission, making OGDCL a cornerstone of the national energy strategy.

What OGDCL Does Well
✓Pakistan's largest E&P company — dominant market position with ~42% oil and ~48% gas production share
✓Consistent revenue growth — CAGR of ~10.6% over 10 years from PKR 181B to PKR 446B
✓Strong free cash flow generation — FCF grew from PKR 36B in FY15 to PKR 66B in FY24
✓Regular dividends — DPS more than doubled from PKR 5.0 (FY15) to PKR 12.0 (FY24)
✓Low leverage — Debt/Equity ratio remains conservative at 0.17x with strong interest coverage
Risks to Watch
⚠Circular debt exposure — PKR 2.5+ trillion inter-corporate debt in Pakistan energy sector affects receivables quality
⚠Commodity price risk — Revenue directly linked to international crude oil prices and domestic gas pricing policy
⚠Government ownership concentration — ~69% GoP stake means business decisions may be influenced by policy priorities
⚠Declining field production — Natural depletion of mature fields requires continuous exploration investment
⚠PKR depreciation sensitivity — International contracts and pricing in USD creates currency translation effects
