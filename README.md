# OGDCL Financial Performance Analysis (2015–2024): A Decade of Resilience

**An analytical review of Pakistan's flagship oil & gas exploration and production company — built and presented as a team-led project, graded and commended in class.**

## About this project

Oil & Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL) is Pakistan's largest exploration and production company, accounting for **46% of domestic oil production** and **28% of natural gas output**. Founded in 1961, it operates as a public limited company with majority government ownership.

This project analyzes a full decade (2015–2024) of OGDCL's financial performance to answer: *how resilient is Pakistan's energy flagship, and what does its financial trajectory signal for investors and stakeholders?*

**I led a 4-person team on this project** — structuring the analysis, building the financial model, and presenting the findings to faculty. The project was graded and appreciated in class.

## Model structure

The Excel model is built across seven linked sheets:
- **Income Statement** — revenue, margins, and profitability over the 10-year window
- **Balance Sheet** — asset base, liabilities, and equity trends
- **Cash Flow** — operating, investing, and financing cash flow analysis
- **Ratio Analysis** — profitability, liquidity, leverage, and efficiency ratios
- **DCF Valuation** — a discounted cash flow view of intrinsic value
- **Data Sources** — sourcing and assumptions documentation

## Key focus areas

- Revenue and profitability trends across a full decade, spanning multiple oil price cycles
- Balance sheet strength and capital structure evolution
- Valuation via DCF to assess whether the stock's market performance aligns with underlying fundamals
- What OGDCL's decade-long trajectory implies about resilience in a state-owned, commodity-exposed business
